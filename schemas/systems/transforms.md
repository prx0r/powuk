# pow-frontier transforms

## breakthrough_materiality

Decompose:
- measured improvement magnitude
- scale demonstrated
- deployment readiness
- cost relevance
- addressable workload share
- independent evidence

Do not convert press-release adjectives into numeric performance.

## seesaw_candidate

Required fields:
- innovation
- constraint_relaxed
- mechanism
- possible_new_constraint
- at least 2 observable predictions
- at least 1 kill condition
- at least 1 target garden metric

## evidence accumulation

Events from other gardens update:
- prediction support
- kill conditions

Frontier itself should not claim success because more papers mention the topic.
